Gear Types We Manufacture

From simple spur gears to complex planetary gear sets, we deliver precision solutions for every application.

Spur & Helical Gears

Standard and helical tooth profiles for power transmission, automotive transmissions, and industrial machinery.

  • Module: 0.5 - 3.0
  • Helix angle: up to 30°
  • Density: 6.8 - 7.2 g/cm³

Planetary Gear Sets

Compact high-ratio gear systems for robotics, reducers, and precision motion control applications.

  • Ratio: 3:1 to 100:1
  • High torque capacity
  • Low backlash design

Specialty & Bevel Gears

Custom bevel gears and specialty profiles for unique power transmission requirements.

  • Straight & spiral bevel
  • Custom tooth profiles
  • Integrated features
