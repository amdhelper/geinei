Case Study #3

American Hydraulic Equipment Manufacturer:
Rescuing a China Assembly Line with Quality PM Couplings

Heavy Equipment Hydraulic Couplings 🇺🇸 USA + 🇨🇳 China Rush Order

A US-based hydraulic equipment manufacturer produces hydraulic cylinders for truck dump beds. When their China assembly plant encountered quality failures with a locally sourced coupling, they turned to Yeh Sheng for an urgent replacement supply — requiring fast turnaround without compromising quality.

The Challenge

Local Supplier Quality Failure

The China assembly plant had been sourcing couplings from a local supplier, but the parts failed to meet the required dimensional accuracy and material consistency — causing assembly issues and risking product reliability.

Urgent Production Timeline

With the assembly line at risk of stopping, the customer needed a qualified replacement supplier who could deliver production-ready parts on a rush schedule — not weeks or months from now, but as fast as possible.

Same Part, Two Continents

The coupling used in the China plant is identical to the one supplied by Yeh Sheng to the US headquarters — so the customer already knew the quality. The challenge was purely about speed and logistics.

The PM Solution

Proven Part, Zero Ramp-Up

Since Yeh Sheng was already producing the identical coupling for the US operations, no new tooling or qualification was needed — the existing tooling, material, and process parameters were applied directly.

Rush Production & Priority Scheduling

Yeh Sheng prioritized the order and expedited production to meet the customer's urgent timeline, demonstrating the flexibility that comes with a responsive, high-mix production facility.

Cross-Region Supply Capability

By supplying the same part to both US and China operations from a single qualified source in Taiwan, the customer consolidated their supply chain and ensured consistent quality across all manufacturing sites.

Key Takeaway

When a local supplier fails, having a trusted PM partner who already produces your part makes all the difference. Yeh Sheng's ability to respond quickly to urgent cross-border requests — with zero ramp-up time and proven quality — turned a potential production crisis into a seamless supply chain expansion.
